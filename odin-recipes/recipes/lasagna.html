Lasagna

Recipe Directions

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb cheese of choice
  • 1 box of lasagna noodles
  • 2 cans of tomato sauce

Instructions

  1. Cook meat until browned
  2. In a separate oven safe bowl, lay down lasagna noodles
  3. Layer cheese, tomato sauce, and then noodles in that order to desired height
  4. Cook for 30 min on 315F
  5. Enjoy
